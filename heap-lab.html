Shape rule

Both heaps are complete binary trees stored as a plain array. For index i, the children sit at 2i+1 and 2i+2, and the parent at (i−1)//2.

Insert

The new value goes into the next free slot, the end of the array. It then swaps with its parent until the heap rule holds again.

Extract

The root is removed and the last value moves into its place. That value sinks, swapping with the child that should be on top, until both children fit the rule.
